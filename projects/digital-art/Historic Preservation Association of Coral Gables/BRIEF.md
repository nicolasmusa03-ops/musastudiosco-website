## Description
The following is a snippit of an email that was sent to me 

"I am working on a study of the Crafts Section that was just approved by our board at the Historic Preservation Association of Coral Gables (HPACG). This is the area where the San Sebastian Apartments are near Le Jeune and University. HPACG is a 501(c)3 non-profit and we would greatly appreciate the help if you know of any students that could do architectural photography and interesting artwork (could be drawings or paintings)? This would be a great way to help us since funding is so scarce these days and also great for their resumes. This a project that could be done on their own time over the next few months working outside and at their own home."

My understanding of the project was to create artisitc renderings of historical buildings throughout Coral Gables, one of Miami's oldest cities, as a favor for HPACGs event that was to be free for the public.

## Goals
I was trying to capture the historical beauty of these buidlings, while also making them visually intersting since they were being digitally painted. I wanted the buildings to have character and a homemade feel.

## Vibe
Historic, architectural, painterly. 

## Notes
This was the first time I ever did anything super architecture heavy.
