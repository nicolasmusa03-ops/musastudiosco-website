## Description
This project was for DND Musica's newist single "12 y Pico." I used pictures of all members and comnposited them together interwoven with the title of the song. They wanted a very retro beachy band vibe and I tried matching that as best as possible. I presented the final artwork on a CD cover mockup to really sell the end product.

## Goals
I was trying to achieve the vibe that they wanted. A beachy band who is laid back but impactful and energetic. 

## Vibe
Retro, energetic.
