# 12 y Pico — DND Musica

The cover art for DND Musica's single *12 y Pico* is built around a single central tension: five real people interwoven with oversized typography, so that neither the band nor the title fully owns the frame. The result is a piece of art that feels like a vintage record store find — bold, sun-bleached, and alive.

The typographic treatment anchors everything. The title letterforms are scaled to fill the entire cover, rendered in a warm red with a thick white stroke, then pushed behind the band members so figures and text occupy the same plane. This layering collapses the usual hierarchy between image and type — you read the name *through* the band, not beneath it. Against a saturated cobalt blue field with a yellow band at the base, the palette pulls directly from early-2000s Latin pop and beachside energy: hot, direct, slightly sun-faded. A subtle grain texture across the whole composition prevents it from reading as digital — it breathes.

The decision to present the final artwork in a jewel case CD mockup was deliberate. It grounds the piece in a specific era of music culture, reinforcing the retro sensibility the client wanted and giving the artwork a physical weight that a flat digital export never could. The mockup functions as the final design surface — not just presentation.
