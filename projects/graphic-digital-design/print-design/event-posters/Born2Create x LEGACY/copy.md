# Born2Create x LEGACY

## Tagline
Creativity and discipline, sharing the same floor.

## Overview
When Born2Create partnered with LEGACY — a gym — the design challenge was real: how do you honor two communities that seem to operate in completely different registers? Artists and athletes, creative instinct and physical discipline. The answer was to find the place where those two worlds already agree: a commitment to showing up and doing the work.

## Design Process
I made a hard typographic choice first and let everything else follow. "BORN2CREATE" at the top — all caps, maximum weight, white-on-black — wasn't chosen just for impact. It was chosen because it had to be unignorable. When you're bridging two audiences who might not naturally cross over, the headline has to arrest everyone equally.

The LEGACY logotype presented a specific design problem I genuinely enjoyed solving. Fitting their branded pitbull mascot icon into the letter O of "LEGACY" wasn't just a clever visual device — it was a statement about how integrated these two identities were for this event. The pitbull, a symbol of tenacity and raw energy, sits at the literal center of their name. The result reads as LEGACY but holds the brand's icon without any compromise to either element.

Between the two headlines, a halftone-treated photograph of gym equipment — rendered at high contrast until the machinery becomes almost architectural — fills the negative space with texture and energy. It's documentary without being literal; it communicates the physical world of LEGACY without feeling like a stock photo insert.

The body copy block at the bottom breaks from the typographic aggression above it. Set in a smaller, condensed serif, it addresses both audiences directly and without condescension: "Whether you're here to break a sweat, network, or just vibe — this is the space where wellness and creativity become one." The event details and sponsor logos are handled in a strict single-line grid that keeps the bottom from competing with the drama above.

## Design Principles Applied
- **Typographic hierarchy at maximum scale** — weight and size do all the hierarchical work in a monochrome environment
- **Negative space as texture** — halftone photography fills without distracting, adding depth while staying subordinate to type
- **Brand integration** — embedding LEGACY's mascot into the wordmark creates unity rather than just co-branding
- **Audience-aware copy placement** — the body copy speaks explicitly to both communities, making neither feel like an afterthought
