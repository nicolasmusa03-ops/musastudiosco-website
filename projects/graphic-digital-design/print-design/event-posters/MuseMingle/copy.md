# MuseMingle

## Tagline
Reunion energy, Miami summer palette.

## Overview
MuseMingle brought together alumni from Muse — a creative workshop for aspiring models — to reconnect and share what they'd learned since leaving. The event was hosted during Miami Swim Week, which meant the design needed to hold its own in a visual environment already saturated with sun-soaked imagery and fashion energy. It needed to feel celebratory, sophisticated, and effortlessly Miami without disappearing into the noise.

## Design Process
The color came first. Bubblegum pink as the full-bleed background color was a decisive, committed choice — not a safe one. Pink at that saturation and field size demands confidence, but it immediately signals warmth, celebration, and femininity without relying on a single predictable swimwear cliché. It's the kind of color that commands attention at the size a flyer gets printed and doesn't compete with the photography layered into the lower half of the composition.

The typographic system is a deliberate tension between two extremes. "MUSE" is set in a massive, heavy sans-serif display face — loud, architectural, almost confrontational in its scale — while "Mingle" drops in below it in a flowing, elegant script. That pairing is the design's central argument: the workshop is rigorous and serious (MUSE), but the reunion is loose and joyful (Mingle). Neither typeface could carry the poster alone; together they define the event's character.

The yellow on the headline creates a third point in the color triangle (pink field, dark photography, yellow type) that keeps the composition from flattening. The sticker-style callouts for the address, date, and time are a deliberate informality — they read like someone pressed a label onto a mood board. This visual register matches how the Muse community actually communicates: personal, tactile, DIY-adjacent even when things are polished.

The background photography — a sun-drenched poolside image — bleeds naturally from below the typographic section, grounding the aspirational type in a real, physical moment. Rather than framing the photo or cropping it tight, letting it drift into the lower half gives the poster a sense of leisure and ease. The sponsor logos at the bottom are treated as a clean strip — FRUGA, HGAB, Miami Pattern, Oli G — organized in a single line that maintains hierarchy without cluttering the composition above.

## Design Principles Applied
- **Color as personality** — bubblegum pink isn't a background; it's a brand statement that sets the entire emotional register
- **Typographic tension** — pairing a heavy display sans with a flowing script creates a dual identity that mirrors the event itself
- **Sticker-style hierarchy** — informal callout shapes keep event logistics from feeling bureaucratic
- **Photography as atmosphere** — the poolside image grounds the poster in physical reality without competing with the typographic system above it
