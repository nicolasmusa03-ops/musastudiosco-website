## Description
This was a photo retouching project for @byjosemusa, a photographer for Miami known for his expressive and bold photography. He was shooting Amaya Espinal for a Galore Magazine shoot. I was tasked with photoshopping her leaning on a giant papaya, given her nickname "Amaya Papaya." 

## Goals
I was trying to achieve a very hyperealisitc effect with this edit, almost as if there were actual giant lifesize papayas in our real world. 

## Vibe
The vibe was editorial and polished yet still expressive and bold in its character.

## Notes
This project actually forced me to tune up my skills with shadows and perspectives as I had to make sure the papaya looked real within the same space as Amaya.
