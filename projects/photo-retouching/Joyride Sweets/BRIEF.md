## Description
This project was very specific. I was tasked with a few retouches on these photos taken for Joyride's 50 States Challenge campaign, taken by Jose Muse, a miami photographer. I was tasked with retouching the number on the model's jersey to a 50, to match the campaigns messaging, as well as changing the color striped on the jersey to match the blue of Joyrides brand identity. There was also a specific picture in which I edited a plane in the sky writing out "joyride" with air trails. 

## Goals
This was a fun and light hearted edit, yet I still wanted the changes to go unnoticed, as if they were there to begin with. It was important to me that the Jersey felt like it was numbered 50 from the start. The writing in the clouds needed to feel tangible. And the colors needed to match the company's image.

## Vibe
Fun, carefree, wild, go with the flow, happy, stress free.

## Notes
This was more of a straightforward and focused edit but it definitely highlights my skill with attention to detail and those small things you think no one looks at.
