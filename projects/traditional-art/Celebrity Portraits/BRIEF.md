## Description
This was my most popular series on social media. I would choose a celebrity with a lot of traction and create their portraits using graphite/charcoal pencils and a mix of color with colored pencils. This series was meant to be both portfolio worthy but also a personal growth series as well to improve my skills with every portrait.

## Goals
I was trying to achieve precise anatomy and resemblance, while also introducing elements that would capture the eyes of the audience so that they're not just looking at a familiar face but one in a new light. 

## Vibe
Light, fresh, new, familiar, innovative.

## Notes
Present this project in an automatic rotating carousel so that the viewer can read the consise description while all the portraits slide by in their view. I dont want to take up a lot of space. Keep it minimal and to the point.
